Customer-Specific Shipping Discount

Configure customer-specific Shipping Rate Discount that are automatically applied to shipping charges.

Configure customer-specific Sales Order Discount that are automatically applied to quotations and sales orders.

Enhanced Shipping Cost Calculation

Calculate accurate shipping costs using Insurance Fees, Fuel Surcharges, and Remote Area Surcharges.

Sales Order Dashboard

View Today Sales, Weekly Sales, Monthly Sales, Pending Deliveries, Overdue Sale Orders and New Customers This Month from a centralized dashboard.

Sales Order Advance Payment

Register advance payments directly from confirmed sales orders for better payment tracking.

Advanced Sales Order Filters

Filter sales orders using Today, This Week, This Month, This Quarter, and Unshipped Sales Order filters for faster order management.

01 Navigate to Contacts → Sales & Purchase and configure the Shipping Rate Discount and Sale Order Discount percentages for the customer. These discounts are automatically applied when creating sales orders and calculating shipping charges for the selected customer.

02 When a customer with a configured Sale Order Discount is selected the discount percentage is automatically applied to the order lines.

03 Navigate to Configuration → Delivery Methods and configure the Margin on Rate for the delivery method. The configured margin percentage is automatically added to the carrier's shipping cost.

04 Click Add Shipping to calculate the delivery cost. The system adds the configured Delivery Margin, Insurance Fee, Fuel Surcharge, and Remote Area Surcharge to the shipping cost, then applies the customer's Shipping Rate Discount.

05 The final delivery charge displayed on the quotation or sales order is automatically calculated based on the configured shipping fees, delivery margin, and customer shipping discount

06 The Register Payment button is displayed on confirmed Sales Orders, enabling users to register customer payments directly from the order.

07 Click the Register Payment button to record a payment directly from the Sales Order.

08 The dashboard displays Today Sales, Weekly Sales, Monthly Sales, Pending Deliveries, Overdue Sale Orders and New Customers This Month. This gives users a quick overview of sales performance and order status from a single screen.

09 Navigate to Sales → Configuration → Settings and configure the Pending Deliveries Period (Days) and Overdue Sales Orders Period (Days). These settings determine the number of recent days considered when calculating Pending Deliveries and Overdue Sales Orders in the Sales Dashboard

10 Users can view all monthly sales orders, helping them monitor sales performance, review customer transactions, analyze order values, and track business growth throughout the month.

11 Additional sales order filters are available to help users quickly analyze and manage orders. Filter records by Today, This Week, This Month, This Quarter, and Unshipped Sale Orders to easily track sales performance and identify orders that still require delivery processing.

What charges are included in the shipping cost calculation?

The shipping cost can include Insurance Fees, Fuel Surcharges, and Remote Area Surcharges are included in shipping cost.

When is the Register Advance Payment option available?

The Register Advance Payment option becomes available after a quotation is confirmed as a Sales Order.

Can different customers have different Shipping Rate Discounts and Sales Order Discounts?

Yes, both Shipping Rate Discounts and Sales Order Discounts can be configured separately for each customer.

Which sales order filters are included with the module?

The module provides Today, This Week, This Month, This Quarter, and Unshipped Sales Order Products filters for easier sales order management.
